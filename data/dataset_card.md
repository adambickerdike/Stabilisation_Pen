# Dataset card: synthetic disturbance-predictor windows

> **Evidence status: SIMULATION / synthetic data.** Every sample is generated by project code
> from parametric models. No sample is a recording of a person, a patient or a bench rig.
> Nothing here supports a clinical claim.

## 1. What one sample is

- **Input:** 64 housing page-displacement increments Δp_H (x, y) in µm at 250 Hz (256 ms, oldest first).
- **Input:** the Kalman phase-rate estimate f_est (Hz) at the newest tick.
- **Target:** the true housing disturbance d (x, y) in µm at t_k + 6 ms.
- **Labels:** tremor envelope, pen-down at the target time, the segment's nominal tremor f0 and its band.
- **Metadata:** writer profile, tremor segment parameters, sensor parameters and seed.

**Time-stamp convention (ours; see the proposed contract changes in `ml/README.md`):** tick k is stamped t_k when it becomes available to the firmware. It measures the housing at t_k − τ, where τ is the sensor latency (2–3 ms). The target is therefore 8–9 ms ahead of the physical time of the newest sample.

## 2. Generation

### Intended motion (per writer)

Recordings are concatenated 6–12 s chunks. Each chunk is either:

- **Handwriting:** sigma-lognormal handwriting (`lognormal_handwriting`).
  - letter height 2.5–7 mm; slant 60–90°; advance 0.4–0.7 × height;
  - 4–11 strokes per word; word gap 0.15–0.4 s;
  - time-scale ("speed") factor 1.0–2.1, log-uniform.
- **Deliberate features:** randomised features built with `PathBuilder`, 10–35 % of chunks.
  - sharp-corner polygons, dots;
  - deliberate hatching at 2.5–6 Hz (inside the tremor band);
  - loops, and fast strokes of 6–15 mm in 0.1–0.2 s.

### Disturbance (per writer)

About 15 % of writers never have tremor. The others have a writer-level `TremorSpec`:

- f0 uniform in 4–12 Hz. The 7–8 Hz band is excluded except in the frequency-holdout split.
- peak amplitude 0.1–0.8 mm, log-uniform.
- f_jitter 0.1–0.5 Hz; f_tau 0.8–3 s; AM depth 0.1–0.5; AM tau 0.4–1.5 s.
- harmonic 0–0.3; ellipticity 0.1–0.9; orientation 0–π.
- Bursts in 30 % of writers (0.05–0.3 /s, 0.1–0.4 mm).
- A broadband 3–12 Hz part in 20 % of writers (5–20 µm RMS).

Tremor is switched on in segments of 4–12 s, separated by tremor-free writing of 2–9 s, with 0.3 s raised-cosine ramps. Per segment, f0 moves by N(0, 0.15) Hz and the amplitude is scaled by 0.7–1.3. This lets realised segment peaks fall slightly outside 0.1–0.8 mm; the observed range is 0.076–0.98 mm.

### Housing and sensor model

- **Housing (kinematic):** p_H = intended + d. The housing follows the hand; there is no grip, contact or friction dynamics in the main splits.
- **Sensor (optical page displacement):**
  - latency τ uniform in 2–3 ms per recording;
  - white position noise 2.5–3.5 µm RMS per axis per sample;
  - scale error ±1 %;
  - sampled at 250 Hz; the predictor sees increments.
- **f_est:** produced by `ml/baselines.py::kf_run`, a verbatim port of `sim/pensim/core.py::_kf_step` plus its phase-rate adaptation, run at 250 Hz.
  - It uses the *assertive* frozen controller configuration from `results/sim/estimator_selection.json`, snapshotted in the manifest at build time: qj 0.1, qt 1e-8, w0 7 Hz, r = 2.5e-11 × 0.5/4.
  - The *balanced* configuration was not used: it never adapts its frequency, because its oscillator amplitude stays below the 20 µm adaptation threshold.
  - A 2 kHz-to-250 Hz conversion of r keeps the continuous-time noise density.

**Stress sets.**

- `stress_tremor`:
  - f_jitter 0.6–1.0 Hz; f_tau 0.3–0.8 s; AM depth 0.5–0.8; AM tau 0.2–0.5 s;
  - harmonic 0.3–0.5 with a *random* harmonic phase (training: fixed 0.7 rad);
  - a broadband part of 20–50 µm in every writer.
- `stress_writing`:
  - speed factor 2.1–2.6; letter height 5–8 mm; 50 % deliberate-feature chunks;
  - median 50 % of intended velocity energy in 4–7 Hz.

**Realism set.** `sim/pensim` scenarios `handwriting(seed, 10 s)` (seeds 300–311, disjoint from the simulator's tuning seeds 100–105 and test seeds 200–211) run in `neutral` mode.

- Housing disturbance = p_H(tremor run) − p_H(clean run), recorded at 2 kHz.
- The same sensor model as the main splits is applied.
- 40–68 % of this disturbance's power lies below 3 Hz: a slow path divergence created by the tremor through contact and hand dynamics. Evaluation therefore also reports the 3–15 Hz part, which is how `sim/pensim/evaluate.py` scores its band error.

## 4. Realism checks against the literature (all weak)

| Quantity | Synthetic (train, median [IQR]) | Literature | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share of intended velocity energy in 4–7 Hz | 0.17 [0.11, 0.36] | ~0.17 | CON-25: one writer, isolated large characters (derived) |
| Share in 8–12 Hz | 0.037 [0.024, 0.051] | 0.013–0.017 | CON-25 |
| Mean pen-down speed | 23 mm/s [18, 29] | 30 ± 8 mm/s | CON-20: phrase writing |
| Tremor amplitude at the nib | 0.1–0.8 mm peak (design range) | no nib data; ET spirals, geometric mean 2 mm p-p | PDT-12 |

Two caveats:

- The speed distribution was chosen so that the median 4–7 Hz share matches CON-25. That is a single writer and cannot anchor a population.
- The synthetic writing has 2–3× more 8–12 Hz energy than CON-25. This makes the high-frequency bands slightly harder.

## 5. Leakage controls (all pass; `results/ml/dataset_manifest.json` → `leakage_checks`)

- Writer ids are disjoint across all splits; seed spawn keys are unique.
- Intended paths are unique across splits (content SHA-256).
- No train, val or test tremor segment has its nominal f0 in 7–8 Hz. Every holdout segment does.
- 3.9 % of training tremor samples still have an *instantaneous* frequency in 7–8 Hz, because of frequency wander. The holdout is a nominal-f0 holdout, not a clean spectral one.
- The canonical feature course appears only in `test_features`.
- Realism seeds are disjoint from the simulator's tuning and test seeds.
- Input scaling constants are fixed a priori, not fitted to data.
- The target lies strictly after the input window. Unit tests check that the output is invariant to inputs after the newest sample and before the window.

## 6. Known biases and gaps (why this data cannot decide anything clinical)

1. **One generator family.** Tremor is a narrowband oscillator with a sinusoidal waveform and a harmonic at a fixed phase. Writing is sigma-lognormal strokes plus minimum-jerk features. A learned model can exploit these regularities; see the stress results in `ml/README.md`.
2. **Kinematic housing.** In the main splits there is no grip compliance, friction, stick-slip or pen-lift dynamics. The realism set shows how much this matters.
3. **No impaired writing.** There is no micrographia, bradykinesia, freezing, variable pressure or pathological stroke shapes. There is no tremor–voluntary interaction such as task-specific or entrained tremor.
4. **Idealised sensing.**
   - The optical signal is always valid, including while hovering.
   - Noise is white; there are no drop-outs, paper-texture errors or tilt-dependent scale errors.
   - f_est comes from our 250 Hz port, not the firmware's 2 kHz estimator.
5. **Writing direction prior.** Lines always advance along +x of the page frame, and the model may use this.
6. **Tremor presence statistics.** On/off segments with about 55 % duty are a design choice. Real users with tremor may have tremor whenever they write.

## 7. What real data replaces this (see `ml/README.md` §4)

- Instrumented passive-pen recordings (EXP-H01), with ethics approval.
- Bench recordings with injected housing disturbance, which provide supervised labels (EXP-B09).
- Real samples keep this schema, with `evidence_status` = `measured` or `bench (injected disturbance)`.
- For free human writing, `tremor` and `target.d_um` are null, because no ground-truth tremor exists.

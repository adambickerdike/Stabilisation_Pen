Stabilisation Pen

An actively stabilising pen for essential tremor, Parkinson's, poor handwriting and dyslexia, with AI note capture. Everything here is design, calculation and simulation: nothing has been built or tested on people yet.

3. The latest studies (29 September 2026)

  • Whole pen in the physics simulator: results per condition on simulated writers and tremor; why the nose uses too much power.
  • Real recorded handwriting and tremor: on real tremor no tracker helps yet; with perfect knowledge of the tremor the same pen would make 7 of 10 words readable.
  • Spelling help, word completion and clearer writing: the spell checker, the physical cues, and why close tracing and shape assist do not make letters clearer.
  • Measurement rigs: the six bench rigs to build first, with CAD, software and parts.
  • Response to the independent review, with the review itself.
  • Decision log and validation plan (requirements, experiments, pass/fail criteria).

Still running when this was written: the balanced nib (study B), shifting the whole pen (study W) and tremor estimation on real data (study E). Their results arrive in later versions.

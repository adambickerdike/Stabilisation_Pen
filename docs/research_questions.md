# Highest-risk research questions (ranked)

**Ranking rule:** each question is scored by the design decisions it can change, weighted by the cost of being wrong. It is not scored by ease of answering (brief §4). Each question names the decisive experiment and the decision it gates. Experiment protocols are in [`validation/bench_protocols.md`](../validation/bench_protocols.md).

| Rank | Question | Current evidence | Why it is high risk | Decisive experiment | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What transverse load does the nib carry during real writing, as a function of force, altitude, direction, ink and paper? | Analytical N·cos θ + μN (COR-01); literature N ≈ 0.8 N normal, μ 0.09–0.4 (CON) | Sets actuator size, heat and configuration (B vs D vs E). The error is squared in the power | **EXP-B01**: refill drag and reaction map on a 3-axis force sensor, 0.2–4 N, 35–80°, 8 directions, 3 papers, 3 inks, 1–100 mm/s, plus 3–15 Hz reciprocation | DEC-003/008 configuration; actuator freeze |
| 2 | How large is tremor at the nib during natural writing, per axis and in mm, for ET, PD and older adults? | ET spirals: geometric mean 2 mm p-p (PDT-12); no nib data | Sets travel, addressable population and the whole clinical claim | **EXP-H01**: instrumented passive pen (IMU + optical flow + force, µm-class reference on coded paper), n ≈ 20 per group, after ethics approval | REQ-USR-002; REQ-MECH-001 |
| 3 | Can intended motion and tremor be separated causally in the 4–8 Hz overlap band, by any method? | Sim: conventional estimators help only above ~8 Hz (COR-11); spectrum data CON-25 | Decides whether free-writing assistance is a product feature or only guided tasks | **EXP-E01**: causal estimator bake-off on EXP-H01 recordings (Kalman, BMFLC, TCN) at matched distortion | REQ-CTRL-007; ML go/no-go (REQ-ML-001) |
| 4 | Does loaded cancellation improve deposited ink at practical force within the power and heat budget? | Sim oracle 0.21–0.27 of powered-neutral error; no hardware | The report's first decisive experiment; unproven | **EXP-B09**: bench rig with injected housing disturbance, µm metrology, ink scans | Phase B → C gate |
| 5 | Is the ink tolerant of the pressure modulation that correction produces (~0.1 N RMS at 3–15 Hz)? And what hand normal compliance (γ) do real grips present? | Sim Nmod 0.07–0.14 N; hand impedance measured in-plane only | Axial-path stiffness trade-off (COR-04/05) | **EXP-B08** (ink vs modulated force) and **EXP-B06** (grip normal impedance, γ identification) | DEC-006; REQ-MECH-004/005 |
| 6 | Can a chip-scale optical sensor near a moving nib give ≤ 3 ms, ≤ 5 µm-per-sample motion on paper at 35–75° and any roll? | DeltaPen 24–68 µm per 10 ms on Wacom; P3040 under NDA | Without it, only external or coded references work | **EXP-S01**: optical latency and accuracy rig (LED-strobe latency, paper and tilt sweep) | REQ-SNS-001; research-pen sensing choice |
| 7 | Is a skid-referenced constant-force nib acceptable for writing feel and smear, and does its friction passively reduce tremor? | Trade: 0.07 W vs 0.48 W (config D vs B) | Could remove the thermal and runtime problem | **EXP-H03**: blinded feel and smear study with skid prototypes (non-active), n ≈ 12 | DEC-008 product baseline |
| 8 | Does assisted practice (fading guidance, error amplification) improve unassisted handwriting, or impair it? | HAP-09 shows harm from error-removing guidance | Separates the training claim from the assistance claim | **EXP-H04**: matched-practice RCT with delayed retention and transfer | Training-mode claims |
| 9 | Can a compact actuator reach K_m ≥ 0.3 N/√W per axis with ±1.6 mm stroke inside Ø14.6 mm without overheating a moving coil? | Field model 0.24–0.34 (flat proxy); thermal 130 K/W air gap | Rev A may not be buildable at spec | **EXP-B03**: force–current–position map and thermal test of actuator coupons (moving coil vs moving magnet) | Actuator freeze; REQ-ACT-* |
| 10 | Which intended-path features must be preserved (corners, dots, hatching), and what distortion can users perceive? | No direct data | Sets the distortion limits used in tuning | **EXP-H05**: perception thresholds for ink distortion (blinded pairs) | REQ-CTRL-005 |
